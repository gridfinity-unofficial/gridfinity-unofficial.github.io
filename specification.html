Magnets

These can be used to supplement the socket and secure blocks in baseplates more reliably. The magnets are 6mm diameter, 2mm thickness. Blocks should ideally have holes in all four corners of every unit to ensure compatibility in all rotations.

Use M3 screws in the corners.

All magnets should be installed with the same polarity so blocks work in every rotation. By convention, the north pole faces up: looking down at a baseplate you see north poles, and looking at the underside of a block you see south poles. Baseplates that use steel (screws, washers or a steel sheet) instead of magnets are unaffected by polarity.

To identify north, lay a stack of magnets on its side in a cup floating in a bowl of water. The face that turns toward geographic north is the magnet's north pole; mark it. (Earth's geographic North Pole is a magnetic south pole, which is why it attracts the magnet's north.)

Tip: an insertion tool such as EmGi's Magnet Insertion Tool v2 (also on MakerWorld) helps keep polarity consistent. Print two: a red one that leaves the north pole showing (for baseplates) and a blue one that leaves the south pole showing (for the bottom of bins).

Labels

Blocks can optionally have labels for easier organisation of your containers. They also provide a lip making it easier to pick up individual blocks.
