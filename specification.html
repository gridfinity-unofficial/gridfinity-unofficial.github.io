Magnets

These can be used to supplement the socket and secure blocks in baseplates more reliably. The magnets are 6mm diameter, 2mm thickness. Blocks should ideally have holes in all four corners of every unit to ensure compatibility in all rotations.

All magnets should be installed with the same polarity. By Convention north pole up: north faces up on baseplates, south faces down on blocks. Steel baseplates are unaffected.

To find north, float a stack of magnets on its side in a cup in water; the face pointing to geographic north is the magnet's north pole. (Earths Georaphic north is magnetic south)

EmGi's Magnet Insertion Tool v2 (6x2mm) (pritables) (MakerWorld) helps keep polarity consistent: print one in red for baseplates (north showing) and one in blue for blocks (south showing).

Alternatively use M3 screws in the corners.

Labels

Blocks can optionally have labels for easier organisation of your containers. They also provide a lip making it easier to pick up individual blocks.
